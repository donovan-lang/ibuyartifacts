Frequently Asked Questions

How long does the appraisal take?
Most appraisals are completed within 48 hours. Complex pieces or those requiring additional research may take up to 5 business days. We'll keep you informed of any delays.
Is the appraisal really free?
Yes, completely free with no obligation. We provide appraisals as a service because we're genuinely interested in acquiring quality artifacts. If you choose to sell, that's a separate transaction entirely.
What happens after I receive my appraisal?
Along with the valuation, you'll receive a purchase offer if we're interested in the piece. You're free to accept, negotiate, or decline — there's zero pressure. The appraisal is yours to keep regardless.
Can I get a physical in-person appraisal?
For high-value pieces or collections, we can arrange an in-person evaluation. We work with accredited appraisers across the US and can travel to your location for significant collections.
Is my information kept confidential?
Absolutely. We take discretion seriously. Your personal information, artifact details, and transaction records are kept strictly confidential and never shared with third parties.
