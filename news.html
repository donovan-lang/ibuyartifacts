Massive Roman Villa Complex Found Beneath London

Construction workers uncovered an extensive Roman villa dating to the 2nd century AD during excavation for a new office building in the City of London. The complex includes intact mosaic floors depicting mythological scenes, a sophisticated hypocaust heating system, and a private bathhouse — suggesting the owner was among the wealthiest residents of Roman Londinium.

Archaeologists from the Museum of London Archaeology (MOLA) describe it as "the most significant Roman discovery in the capital in over a decade." Hundreds of artifacts have been recovered, including pottery, coins, and personal items that paint a vivid picture of elite Roman-British life.
